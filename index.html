01 · The letter

A letter I should have written sooner

Dear Gathan,

I'm sorry. I really am.

You were always allowed to explore on your own. You didn't do something wrong just because I had pictured the moment differently. I wish I had told you I was just sad instead of taking it out on you.

When you told me you needed time to process everything, I realised that explaining what I meant wasn't the same as undoing how it made you feel. I'm sorry I hurt you, especially when all I wanted was to share something meaningful with you.

I was disappointed. I had imagined getting to show you around a place that is part of my everyday life, and I felt like I'd missed that chance. I didn't know how to express that feeling properly in the moment.

I care about you, I care about us and Kaveena now,and I never want that care to become a reason you feel disrespected or uncomfortable around me. You deserved more patience and kindness from me in that moment.

At this point you think I will never change, and maybe that's true cause everyday I feel like I ruin everyones' lives, but I want you to know that I am committed to doing better. I should have spoken calmly and told you why I was disappointed. I should have listened to your side instead of shouting and using words that could hurt you. You deserved that respect, even while I was upset.

I can't promise I'll never make a mistake, but I can be more thoughtful about how I handle my feelings. I am not writing this to make you forgive me quickly, to make you reassure me, or to ask you to decide anything about us. I just wanted to give you a sincere apology and acknowledge what I did wrong.

I'm sorry, Gathan. You don't have to reply before you're ready. I'll respect the time you need.

With care,
Krishna ♡
